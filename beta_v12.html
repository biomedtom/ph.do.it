⚠️ Important: Your project data is not backed up automatically

ph.do.it saves your changes in this browser only. It does not store your project in an online account or automatically create a downloadable backup.

  • You must manually click “Export backup” and keep the downloaded JSON file somewhere safe. Repeat this regularly to save your latest changes.
  • Clearing cookies or website/site data can permanently erase your project from this browser. Clearing browsing history alone does not normally erase it.
  • Projects do not automatically transfer to another browser or device. Use “Import backup” to restore a saved JSON file.
  • Avoid entering confidential or sensitive research information.

This warning appears every time you open or reload ph.do.it.
