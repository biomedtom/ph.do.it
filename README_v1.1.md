# ph.do.it 🌶️

**A free, visual PhD project organiser, built by a PhD student for PhD students.**

[Try ph.do.it](https://biomedtom.github.io/ph.do.it/)

## ⚠️ Important: Your data and privacy

**ph.do.it currently stores your project information locally in your browser, not in an online account.**

- Your work is saved automatically in the browser you're using.
- Closing the website or restarting your computer will not normally delete your work.
- **Clearing browser cookies/site data may permanently delete your project.**
- Your projects will not automatically transfer between different browsers or computers.
- **Regularly export a backup** using the app's backup feature.
- Avoid entering confidential, sensitive or unpublished research information.

There is currently no cloud backup or account recovery system.

## What is ph.do.it?

PhDs are complicated. Experiments depend on other experiments, priorities constantly change, and it's easy to lose track of how individual tasks contribute to the bigger picture.

**ph.do.it** is a free, visual project organiser designed to help PhD students break their research into manageable stages and see how everything fits together.

Rather than another overwhelming productivity platform, the goal is to provide a straightforward place to organise your PhD and figure out what to work on next.

## Features

- Visual project flowcharts
- Progress tracking
- Prioritised Next actions with 🌶️ ratings
- Numbered, dependent task checklists
- Automatic browser-based saving
- JSON backup and restore

## Why I made it

I'm a biomedical PhD student, and I wanted a better way to visualise my research.

Traditional to-do lists don't always capture the dependencies and interconnected stages of experimental work. I wanted something that could show the bigger picture while still making the next small step obvious.

So I started building ph.do.it.
